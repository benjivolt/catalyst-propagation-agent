How the engine learns

  1. Prediction ledgerEvery gate or AI reviewer decision is saved with source, event type, confidence, risk flags, and later evidence needed.
  2. Delayed outcome checksPredictions are rechecked after time passes using saved catalyst/report/source evidence and scheduled outcome checks.
  3. Policy learningSource reputation, confidence calibration, threshold policies, and AI error memory update future scheduler and gate behavior.
